2. Stock Management
This is one of the most important sections.
Stock In
When the business receives new stock:
Select product
Enter quantity
Enter cost price
Supplier
Invoice/reference number
Date
Notes
Add stock


Example:
Coca Cola → +50 bottles → Supplier ABC → Cost $0.50
Stock Out
For manually removing inventory:
Select product
Quantity
Reason
Notes
Date/user
Reasons could be:
Damaged
Expired
Wasted
Lost
Internal use
Adjustment
Stock Adjustment
Admin can manually correct stock.
Example:
System says: 50
 Actual count: 47
 Adjustment: -3
Important: don't simply overwrite the quantity. Create a stock movement record so you have an audit trail.
